# Global TRIZ portfolio — autonomous money mechanisms
Date: 2026-10-21
Scope: worldwide, excluding the founder's prior industry; no advertising strategy used as a core selection criterion; no manual consulting or human fulfillment.

## How the ideas were generated
Methods: TRIZ Ideal Final Result; contradiction analysis; function-oriented search across unrelated industries; inversion; resource analysis; morphological combination; Systematic Inventive Thinking (subtraction, division, task unification, attribute dependency). This is a hypothesis shortlist, not a claim that a separate parallel agent swarm executed; connected research-agent calls were limited.

## Source-checked market evidence (as of 2026-10-21)

- x402 describes an HTTP payment standard where APIs/content can be paid for programmatically without ordinary accounts or API keys: https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402/blob/main/docs/introduction.md
- Santos Website Intelligence currently publishes per-call USDC prices of $0.002 for safe fetch, $0.005 for content extraction, $0.015 for quick intelligence audit, $0.075 for agent-readiness audit and more: https://api.santosautomation.com/docs
- Santos says a quick audit is $0.015 and a full agent-readiness audit is $0.075 per successful call: https://api.santosautomation.com/agent-readiness-audit
- AgentReady offers free audit and paid merchant-side plans of $9/month, $29/month and $79/month, including publishing/monitoring features: https://joinagentready.com/pricing
- Stripe lists standard online card processing at 2.9% + $0.30 per successful domestic-card transaction (international/currency-conversion fees can add more): https://stripe.com/pricing. This is a reminder that low-price consumer-card microtransactions can be uneconomic; x402 removes the fixed 30¢ card component but does not remove infrastructure, tax or compliance cost.
- Open-source pay-per-call agent-payment middleware such as MCPay is already being built: https://github.com/microchipgnu/MCPay. This makes the payment rail an enabling component rather than a defensible product on its own.

## Financial reality: OfferProof
- Proposed price: $0.01 per successfully returned fact packet. This is a hypothesis under the $0.015 generic quick-audit competitor, justified only if the narrow mismatch report is useful.
- 50,000 successfully paid requests = $500 gross, before hosting/network costs/taxes.
- At 1,000 paid requests = $10 gross; at 0 = $0.
- No real customers, transaction volume, settlement or revenue have been observed. Revenue to date is $0.
- The app is not deployed and x402 is disabled until the receiving address/facilitator are configured. This is a built and CI-verified prototype, not an operating business.

## Decision rule
Continue OfferProof only if: (1) public-page fixtures demonstrate a specific mismatch that broad audits do not clearly surface; (2) the report is useful to a real independent agent or developer; (3) a successful testnet payment then a real authorized mainnet settlement can be verified; (4) a repeated paid call occurs without human support. Otherwise test Candidate 2 or 3 with a read-only/dry-run first version, rather than polishing the current concept forever.
